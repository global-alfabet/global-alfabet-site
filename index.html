What is Global Alfabet?

Global Alfabet is a parent technology company that develops and manages digital products while also providing focused digital services to businesses.

What services does Global Alfabet provide?

The company currently focuses on UI/UX design, website development and digital product strategy.

What is BizDirect?

BizDirect is a business directory platform designed to help businesses create a stronger digital presence and help customers find products, services, locations and contact information.

What is Global Alfabet Shop?

Global Alfabet Shop is an online marketplace concept designed to connect customers with products from businesses and independent sellers.
