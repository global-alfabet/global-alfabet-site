An interactive site plan is a version of your estate layout that buyers can open, tap and explore online, instead of reading a static PDF. Alfa Maps builds one in 3D. You send the surveyor's layout plan and your plot list. We trace every plot and turn it into a 3D estate that buyers explore on their phone, with each plot's size, status, price and payment plans, and a way to contact your sales team in one tap.

## What is an interactive site plan?

Most estates are sold from two documents: a black-and-white layout plan and a spreadsheet of plot numbers. Both are accurate. Neither is easy for a buyer to read.

An interactive site plan puts the same information in one place. Each plot can be tapped. It shows whether the plot is for sale, reserved or sold, how big it is, and what it costs. A 3D site plan goes one step further: the buyer sees the plot to scale, next to its neighbours, roads and open spaces, from above or from the street.

[Alfa Maps](https://maps.globalalfabet.com/) is a 3D interactive site plan made for plots, houses and townhouses.

## What do you need to send?

You probably have everything already:

- **The general or layout plan** from your surveyor. A PDF exported from CAD works best. DWG or DXF is fine too.
- **The plot list**, with each plot's number and size, in Excel or CSV. Add the status (for sale, reserved, sold) if you have it.
- **Your prices and payment plans**: price per square metre or per plot, deposit, terms, interest and fees.
- **Your logo**, and photos of the site if you have them.

You upload these in your Alfa Maps dashboard. Only your team and Global Alfabet can see them.

## How does a layout plan become a 3D estate?

Global Alfabet traces every plot from your plan and builds the estate in 3D, with your branding. Plot boundaries, roads and open spaces come from the plan, so plots are shown to scale.

You then review the estate privately. Your team can set each plot to for sale, reserved or sold, add prices, choose the houses shown, and set the sales contact. An owner in your company approves it, or asks us for changes. Nothing is public until an owner chooses to share it.

## What can buyers do in a 3D site plan?

Buyers open a normal web link. There is no app to install and no account to create. It works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers.

In Alfa Maps, a buyer can:

- **Fly over the whole estate**, or switch to the estate map, street, aerial, garden, site plan and free-roam views.
- **See what's available at a glance.** Plots for sale are green, reserved plots amber and sold plots red.
- **Filter plots** by status, size and price, or search for a plot number.
- **Check the facts** of each plot: size, frontage, road width, position and boundary lengths.
- **Try a house on the plot.** There are eight house types, and buyers can change storeys, garage, pool, roof and colours. The house always fits the plot.
- **See the numbers**: cash price, deposit, fees and monthly payments for each payment plan you offer.
- **Download a plot pack**, a branded PDF with the site plan and boundary lengths, views of the plot, the price and payment plans, and who to talk to.
- **Enquire in one tap**, with the plot already filled in.

The houses are concept homes to help buyers picture the finished estate. If you have your own house plans, they can be added as house types.

## How do you keep plot status and prices up to date?

Your team edits the estate in its admin view. Mark a plot sold or reserved, change a price, or update the house shown on a plot, then save. Buyers see the change straight away.

Owners, staff and agents each get their own access. Agents get their own links to shared estates, so enquiries from buyers they send go to them, and plot packs show their contact details.

## How do you share it with buyers?

Once the estate is approved, an owner chooses who can open it: only your team, anyone with the link, or anyone at all, including people who find it on search engines.

There are three ways to put it in front of buyers:

- **A link or button** on your development page, adverts and messages.
- **Inside your own website**, with an embed code you paste into the page.
- **A link to one plot.** Every plot has its own link, so you can send a buyer straight to the plot they asked about.

## Who is it for?

Alfa Maps is for developers selling plots, houses and townhouses. It helps most when buyers can't easily visit the site: families in another city, buyers living abroad, or anyone deciding before roads and houses exist.

It also suits developments sold in phases. Each new phase can be added from the same kind of plans.
