To sell plots online to buyers who can't visit the site, give them what a site visit would: where the plot is, how big it is, what's around it, what it costs, and who to talk to. Put all of it behind one link they can open on their phone and share with family. The clearer the information, the fewer questions stand between a buyer and a decision.

## Who are remote buyers?

Remote buyers are people who want a plot but can't walk the site before they decide. Some live in another city. Many live in another country. The UN counts [304 million international migrants worldwide in 2024](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/content/international-migrant-stock), and some of them want to buy a plot or a home where they grew up or where family still lives. Buyers like these often decide with family members in different places, all looking at the same information on their phones.

For these buyers, a layout plan and a price list sent in a chat are rarely enough. They can't stand on the plot, look down the road or ask the sales agent a quick question face to face.

## What do buyers need to know before they buy a plot?

Think about the questions a buyer asks on a site visit. A good online listing answers each one:

1. **Where exactly is the plot?** Its position in the estate, which way it faces, and what is next to it: roads, open spaces, neighbouring plots.
2. **How big is it?** The size, the frontage, the boundary lengths and the width of the road in front.
3. **Is it still available?** Clear status for every plot, kept up to date. A buyer who falls for a plot that was sold last week loses trust in everything else.
4. **What does it cost, really?** The cash price, the deposit, fees, and the monthly payment for each payment plan you offer.
5. **What could I build there?** A picture of a house on that plot helps buyers imagine the finished home.
6. **Who do I talk to?** A named contact, reachable in the way the buyer already uses.

## Why don't layout plans and price lists work for remote buyers?

A layout plan is made for surveyors and planners. It is accurate, but hard to read on a phone. Plot numbers are small, and there's no sense of scale or of what the estate will look like.

A price list goes out of date the moment a plot is sold. Different versions end up in different chats. A buyer abroad may be looking at prices or availability that changed weeks ago.

Neither shows the buyer the plot itself. And without that, many buyers wait until they can visit, or until someone they trust can visit for them.

## How does an interactive site plan help?

An interactive site plan puts the plan, the plot list and the prices in one place, online, always current.

Buyers tap a plot to see its size, status and price. In a 3D site plan, they also see it to scale next to its neighbours, from above and from the street. They can compare plots without asking you for another document.

For you, there is one thing to keep up to date. When a plot is reserved or sold, you change it once and every buyer sees it.

## Why do plot packs matter?

Remote buyers rarely decide alone. They forward details to a spouse, parents or a lawyer. A plot pack, a short printable summary of one plot, gives everyone the same facts: the site plan with boundary lengths, the plot's size, views of the plot, the price and payment plans, and who to contact.

A buyer can save it, print it and come back to it. It also travels well in a chat, which is where many of these conversations happen.

## How do one-tap enquiries help?

The harder it is to ask a question, the fewer people ask. A one-tap enquiry opens a message to your sales team with the estate and the plot already filled in. The buyer doesn't have to describe which plot they mean, and your team knows straight away what they're asking about.

If agents sell for you, give each of them their own link. Enquiries then reach the agent who sent the buyer.

## What else helps buyers abroad decide?

Some things only you can provide, but they matter just as much:

- **Reply quickly**, and allow for time zones.
- **Explain the buying process in writing**: what happens after a reservation, which documents the buyer receives, and when payments are due.
- **Show real progress** on the site, such as roads and services, with dated photos.
- **Be clear about what is illustration.** 3D houses and estates help buyers picture the finished place. Say so plainly, so buyers know the difference between what exists and what is planned.
